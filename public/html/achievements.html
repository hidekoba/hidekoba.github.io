原著論文 (英文, 査読あり) / Original Articles (English, Peer-Reviewed)

  1. Kobayashi, H. , T. Yokohata, A. Yamamoto, T. Hajima, H. Tatebe, A. Oka, and K. B. Rodgers (2026, Under Review): Multi-centennial ocean biogeochemical responses to extended Shared Socioeconomic Pathways Biogeosciences Discuss . https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-2026-120

    要点 / Key point: 社会経済シナリオを長期間延長した場合に、海洋の生物地球化学的応答が数百年規模でどのように進むかを調べた研究です。 This study examined multi-centennial changes in ocean biogeochemistry under extended Shared Socioeconomic Pathway scenarios.

  2. Kobayashi, H. , A. Oka, T. Obase, and A. Abe-Ouchi (2024): Assessing transient changes in the ocean carbon cycle during the last deglaciation through carbon isotope modeling, Climate of the Past , 20 , 769--787. https://doi.org/10.5194/cp-20-769-2024

    要点 / Key point: 炭素同位体モデリングを用いて、最終退氷期における海洋炭素循環の過渡的変化を評価した研究です。 Carbon-isotope modeling is used to assess transient changes in the ocean carbon cycle during the last deglaciation.

  3. Sasaki, Y., H. Kobayashi , and A. Oka (2022): Global simulation of dissolved 231Pa and 230Th in the ocean and the sedimentary 231Pa/230Th ratios with the ocean general circulation model COCO ver4.0, Geoscientific Model Development , 15 , 2013--2033. https://doi.org/10.5194/gmd-15-2013-2022

    要点 / Key point: 海洋大循環モデル COCO を用いて、溶存 231 Pa・ 230 Th と堆積物中の比を全球規模で再現した研究です。 A global simulation of dissolved 231 Pa and 230 Th and sedimentary 231 Pa/ 230 Th ratios using COCO.

  4. Lhardy, F., N. Bouttes, D. M. Roche, A. Abe-Ouchi, Z. Chase, K. A. Crichton, T. Ilyina, R. Ivanovic, M. Jochum, M. Kageyama, H. Kobayashi , B. Liu, L. Menviel, J. Muglia, R. Nuterman, A. Oka, G. Vettoretti, and A. Yamamoto (2021): A first intercomparison of the simulated LGM carbon results within PMIP-carbon: role of the ocean boundary conditions, Paleoceanography and Paleoclimatology , 36 (10), e2021PA004302. https://doi.org/10.1029/2021PA004302

    要点 / Key point: PMIP-carbon の複数モデルを比較し、最終氷期最盛期の炭素循環再現に海洋境界条件が果たす役割を検討した研究です。 This intercomparison evaluates how ocean boundary conditions influence simulated Last Glacial Maximum carbon-cycle states.

  5. Kobayashi, H. , A. Oka, A. Yamamoto, and A. Abe-Ouchi (2021): Glacial carbon cycle changes by Southern Ocean processes with sedimentary amplification, Science Advances , 7 (35), eabg7723. https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abg7723

    要点 / Key point: 南大洋の物理・生物地球化学過程による氷期炭素循環の変化が、海底堆積物過程によって増幅される可能性を示した研究です。 The study investigates how Southern Ocean processes alter the glacial carbon cycle and how sedimentary processes may amplify those changes.

    ♠ プレスリリース
  6. Kobayashi, H. , and A. Oka (2018): Response of atmospheric pCO2 to glacial changes in the Southern Ocean amplified by carbonate compensation, Paleoceanography and Paleoclimatology , 33 (11), 1206--1229. https://doi.org/10.1029/2018PA003360

    要点 / Key point: 南大洋の氷期的変化に対する大気中 pCO 2 の応答が、炭酸塩補償によって増幅される仕組みを調べた研究です。 This study examines how carbonate compensation amplifies the atmospheric pCO 2 response to glacial Southern Ocean changes.

  7. Kobayashi, H. , A. Abe-Ouchi, and A. Oka (2015): Role of Southern Ocean stratification in glacial atmospheric CO2 reduction evaluated by a three-dimensional ocean general circulation model, Paleoceanography , 30 (9), 1202--1216. https://doi.org/10.1002/2015PA002786

    要点 / Key point: 三次元海洋大循環モデルを用いて、南大洋の成層化が氷期の大気中 CO 2 低下に果たす役割を評価した研究です。 A three-dimensional ocean general circulation model is used to evaluate the role of Southern Ocean stratification in glacial atmospheric CO 2 reduction.
